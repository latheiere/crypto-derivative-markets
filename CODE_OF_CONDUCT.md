# Code of conduct

Project participation must remain respectful, constructive, and safe. Contributors must not engage
in harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, or publication of another person's
private information.

Technical disagreement is welcome when it addresses evidence and behavior rather than people.
Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and interactions that violate these standards,
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel in the public source repository. Reports
will be reviewed with discretion, and information will be shared only as needed to respond fairly.
